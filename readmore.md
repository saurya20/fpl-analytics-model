\# FPL Analytics Model



A personal Fantasy Premier League analytics and decision-support system.



\## Objective



Build an automated FPL model that:



\- Collects historical and current FPL data

\- Predicts player performance

\- Evaluates fixtures and player availability

\- Optimizes transfers and starting XI selection

\- Recommends captaincy

\- Evaluates chips and future FPL "superpowers"

\- Automatically updates after each gameweek



\## Project Status



Currently in Phase 1: Historical Data Foundation.
